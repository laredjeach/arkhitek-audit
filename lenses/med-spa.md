# Niche Lens: Med Spa / Aesthetics

## Business Context

Med spas (medical spas, aesthetic practices, medspa clinics) are high-margin, high-competition local businesses. Botox, fillers, laser treatments, body contouring, and skincare memberships generate strong per-visit revenue. A single Botox appointment is $300–$800; a recurring membership is $150–$400/month.

Decision-makers are typically the medical director (often an NP or MD), practice owner, or spa manager. They are image-conscious — they care deeply about how their brand looks and feels online. They watch competitors obsessively. They're active on Instagram and often have strong social presence but a weaker website.

Key goals:
- New client bookings (especially for high-margin treatments)
- Looking premium, clean, and trustworthy
- Ranking for "[city] Botox," "[city] med spa," specific treatment names
- Appearing in AI search results when people ask "best med spa near me"

## Why Their Website Is Usually Weak

Med spas often over-invest in Instagram and under-invest in their website. The site may look beautiful (good photography, clean design) but be technically hollow: no schema, no FAQ, no AI signals, no review integration.

Or it's the opposite: a functional but generic site built on Wix or Squarespace by a local designer who made it pretty but had no SEO understanding.

Common patterns:
- Heavily visual sites where treatments are listed as single words without descriptions
- No FAQs despite clients asking the same questions about Botox, Juvederm, safety, downtime constantly
- Booking embedded via third-party platform (Mindbody, Vagaro, Acuity) with no local schema tying it to the practice
- No llms.txt; no entity clarity for AI search tools

## ROI Framing

- Botox client × 3 visits/year: $900–$2,400/year
- Membership client: $1,800–$4,800/year
- Laser package client: $2,000–$5,000 upfront
- "If AI search tools don't understand your practice, clients searching 'best Botox in [city]' on ChatGPT or Perplexity won't find you even if you're the best option."
- "Fixing your FAQ schema alone could get you into Google AI Overviews for treatment questions — which is free visibility your competitors aren't getting."

## Common Audit Findings for This Niche

1. **No MedSpa or MedicalBusiness schema** — just a generic LocalBusiness type, if any
2. **No FAQ content** — clients have dozens of questions about Botox, Juvederm, downtime, safety — none of them answered in a crawlable format
3. **No llms.txt** — AI tools have no structured way to understand the practice, services, or credentials
4. **Treatment pages with single-paragraph content** — "We offer Botox." That's not enough for Google or AI to rank you for treatment queries
5. **AggregateRating schema missing** — clients leave reviews but they don't show as stars in search results
6. **Missing medical director / provider credentials on the site** — trust signal that's almost always underdone
7. **Booking platform not integrated with schema** — Vagaro/Mindbody embed doesn't signal to Google that you accept bookings
8. **Social-first, web-second content** — Instagram posts are detailed and engaging; the website is thin by comparison

## Outreach Tone Guidance

- Med spa owners and managers are visually oriented and brand-conscious. Compliment what looks good before suggesting anything.
- Speak in client experience and revenue terms, not technical jargon.
- Avoid: "your canonical tags," "structured data markup," "content gap."
- Use: "when someone searches for Botox in [city] on AI tools like ChatGPT," "what new clients see when they Google your name," "how your reviews appear in search results."
- This niche responds well to: appearing premium, competitive differentiation, AI search as a new channel.
- Email tone: warm, professional, slightly aspirational. Not corporate. Matches the aesthetic of the industry.

## Closing Language That Works

- "Happy to share the full audit — takes 5 minutes to read."
- "If any of this sounds worth discussing, a 15-minute call would cover it."
- "No obligation — I just thought it was worth flagging."

Avoid:
- "Our med spa marketing packages..."
- "We work with hundreds of practices..."
- "Book your free consultation with us..."
