# Template: Follow-Up (Day 3)

## Purpose

A 2-sentence follow-up sent 3 days after the first email. References the first email
by subject. Adds a second observation or asks a simple question. Under 50 words total.

## Rules

- 2 sentences maximum.
- Reference the first email (Re: or "following up on my note about X").
- Add one new angle: a second finding, a question about their situation, or a time-bound observation.
- No re-explaining the first email.
- No apology for following up.

## Template

---

**Subject:** Re: {{ORIGINAL_SUBJECT}}

Hi {{OWNER_FIRST_NAME}},

Following up on my note about {{FIRST_FINDING_SUMMARY}}. {{SECOND_OBSERVATION_OR_QUESTION}}

{{SENDER_NAME}}

---

## Placeholder Reference

| Placeholder | Source |
|---|---|
| `{{ORIGINAL_SUBJECT}}` | Subject from the first email |
| `{{OWNER_FIRST_NAME}}` | Same as first email |
| `{{FIRST_FINDING_SUMMARY}}` | 3-5 word summary of the first email's finding |
| `{{SECOND_OBSERVATION_OR_QUESTION}}` | A second finding from gap_2, or a simple question |
| `{{SENDER_NAME}}` | Same as first email |

## Examples

**Good:**

> Following up on my note about the missing star ratings in your Google listing. Also noticed your contact page returns a 404 — happy to include that in the audit too if you'd like to see it.

> Following up on the homepage title note. Quick question: are you seeing many calls come in from Google searches, or mostly from referrals?

**Bad:**

> I just wanted to follow up on my previous email to see if you had a chance to review it. We're excited about the possibility of working together and would love to schedule a call.
