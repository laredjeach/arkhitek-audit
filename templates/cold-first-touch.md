# Template: Cold First Touch

## Purpose

A short, specific cold email that leads with ONE visible finding from the site audit.
Not a list. Not a pitch. One problem, one impact, one offer.

## Rules

- 3-4 sentences in the body. No more.
- First sentence: the specific finding (visible to the prospect, not jargon).
- Second sentence: the plain-English business impact.
- Third sentence: offer to send the audit.
- Optional fourth sentence: low-pressure close.
- No bullet points. No "I hope this email finds you well." No "We'd love to partner."
- Subject line: max 8 words, specific to this business.

## Template

---

**Subject:** {{SUBJECT_LINE}}

Hi {{OWNER_FIRST_NAME}},

{{PITCH_HOOK}} — {{UPSIDE}}.

I put together a short audit for {{BUSINESS_NAME}} and found a few more things along those lines. Happy to send it over if useful.

Best,
{{SENDER_NAME}}
{{SENDER_COMPANY}}

## Examples of Good vs. Bad Openings

**Good:**
> Your attorney page title currently reads "Crimina Defensel" — Google shows this spelling error in search results to anyone who searches your firm's name.

> Your homepage lists seven services but none of them have pricing or booking links — visitors who want to book have to call, which is friction most won't take on.

> I noticed your Google Business Profile shows 47 reviews but they don't appear as stars in your search listing — that's a missing schema block that's a one-hour fix.

**Bad:**
> Your website's SEO needs optimization to drive more organic traffic to your business.

> We specialize in helping businesses like yours improve their digital marketing.

> I wanted to reach out about your website and see if you'd be interested in our services.
